Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a landscape enemy concept approval sheet for an isometric dark fantasy arena survival game, Couch Crypt. The previous design failed because it was literally an ordinary bronze church bell floating in the air. HARD RESET: this must read instantly as a threatening NONHUMAN MONSTER, an actual living crypt predator, not an object or possessed prop.

Creature: THE MOURNING BELL. A compact hovering grotesque crypt beast, broadly squat pear-shaped body, a thick dark desiccated hide with a few large weathered bone armor plates forming a dome over its upper body. One intensely glaring recessed amber eye set high in the front of its body. Its entire lower front is an enormous horizontal predatory maw with a heavy hinged lower jaw, a few large irregular ivory fangs and deep dark burgundy gums. The mouth is its dominant feature, eye smaller. A rounded ribbed throat under the eye suggests an internal resonating organ, subtly amber lit deep in the throat when roaring; the name comes from its deathly tolling cry, NOT a literal metal bell. Short blunt bone ridges on shell, no elaborate horns. Strong asymmetry in cracked bone plates, controlled and consistent. Big masses, compact silhouette, readable at small gameplay size. No human anatomy, humanoid posture, skull head, arms, legs, hands, wings, tentacles, trailing fabric, chains, jewelry, suspension loop or clapper. NOT a church bell. NOT an ordinary object with a face pasted on. Not cute or comic. No massive tongue or saliva strands. A believable organic nightmare predator with dark flesh visibly integrated between bone plates, unmistakably hostile.

Composition: dark quiet matte blue-grey concept background. Left two thirds one large complete creature hovering close above a small patch of cool-grey crypt paving, showing open teeth and alert predatory gaze. True elevated three-quarter isometric GAMEPLAY view, looking DOWN from above enough to see upper shell, not a low-angle illustration. Right third THREE smaller studies of the same exact creature and same shell design, vertically arranged with ample spacing: "IDLE" mouth almost closed but fangs visible, "LUNGE" body tilted forward with jaw open, "TOLL" jaw dropped roaring with modest amber throat light and ONE fine 2:1 elliptical ground ripple beneath feet level. No limbs added in any pose. Consistent rigid upper body and single jaw hinge: easy to animate with hover, tilt, jaw open/close and recoil. Main creature has no VFX.

Title at top exactly "THE MOURNING BELL". Small subtitle "CRYPT PREDATOR • REVISED CONCEPT". Labels exactly "IDLE", "LUNGE", "TOLL". Restrained old gold typography, clean and legible. No additional captions.
Style: original hand-painted production enemy art for a grounded medieval crypt game, broad painterly shadows, crisp edges and strong readable shapes, aged ivory bone over charcoal-brown leathery flesh, amber eye and throat, limited muted burgundy gums. Creature feels dangerous through anatomy, weight and pose, not through excessive visual effects. Quiet backdrop, no cinematic fog, no flame, no gore splashes, no scenery clutter, no UI or healthbars. Polished painted monster concept art, NOT photoreal, NOT chibi, NOT cartoon mimic chest, NOT literal bell.
