# Survivor-inspired expansion art

Original Couch Crypt artwork generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-11-25. Vampire Survivors supplies mechanics references only; none of its downloaded artwork is shipped here.

`skill-sheet-initial.png` is the first generated master. `skill-sheet-transparent.png` is the selected background-extracted master; a follow-up imagegen edit removed its unwanted background haze while retaining the twelve emblems and their layout. `world-glyph-sheet.png` supplies four separate world sprites. `legacy-skill-icons.png` preserves the exact existing normalized twelve-icon RGBA image.

`scripts/PackSurvivorExpansion.py` crops authored source cells, trims transparent margins and downsamples with Lanczos into padded 64-pixel cells. It copies the original twelve 128-pixel cells unchanged into the top 512x384 area. New skills 12–23 occupy the first twelve cells of an eight-column/two-row strip beneath them; its last four cells contain the sentinel sword, returning glaive, Soul Harvest and Stasis sprites. The full managed runtime/phone atlas is 512x512 RGBA, 1,048,576 GPU bytes (+262,144 bytes), with zero retained decoded Pixmap. Original masters are not loaded by the game.

The current combined application allocation including a 360x360 QR is 33,483,596 bytes (31.93245 MiB), leaving 70,836 bytes below the 32 MiB ceiling. This is accounted texture allocation, not driver/process memory or proof of physical-TV performance.

Rebuild: `python3 scripts/PackSurvivorExpansion.py`. The pinned legacy UI exporter invokes this packer after recreating its original outputs.

## Skill sheet generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production sprite sheet for the original dark-fantasy game Couch Crypt. Create exactly TWELVE separate painted ability emblems in a perfectly even FOUR COLUMN by THREE ROW grid, landscape 4:3 canvas, genuinely transparent background. Each emblem centered within its own equal square cell with generous transparent padding, no overlap, no text, no letters, no numbers, NO enclosing medallions, NO outer frames or badge discs. High-contrast hand-painted medieval fantasy style, weathered iron and antique gold, restrained ivory/teal magic, readable at128px. Ordered left to right top to bottom: row1 (1) a gold arcane crossbow bolt bending along a short curved ricochet trace, (2) ONE upright spectral iron longsword with ivory edge and gold hilt, (3) a compact shallow-carved gold circular Gothic consecration seal seen from above with transparent centre, (4) ONE crescent iron throwing glaive with gold edge. Row2 (5) a single iron-and-gold kite shield with a tiny ivory soul light, (6) a crystalline ivory/teal frost shard with short tiny chips, (7) a gold crossbow bolt with two short gold speed strokes, (8) an antique gold hourglass holding ivory sand. Row3 (9) a branching gold lightning strike with a small paler echo bolt, (10) three small teal binding chains encircling a hollow centre, (11) one gold arcane bolt bursting into a few ivory crystal splinters, (12) two parallel compact golden sword crescents, the second fainter. Maintain distinct silhouettes. These must be original illustrated assets, not logos from any existing game. Output one clean flat sprite sheet, transparency between/around all glyphs, no shadows cast outside each cell. Keep glyph size consistent and large enough to read. No solid black background, no checkerboard painted into image.

## Background extraction prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Edit only the background of this twelve-emblem sprite sheet: remove the large cloudy ivory/grey haze behind the top row and all diffuse background haze anywhere on the canvas. All empty space must have alpha exactly zero. Preserve every existing illustrated weapon/emblem, their exact positions, colors, proportions and the four-column three-row layout. Keep only glyph objects and their small intentional magical traces. Do not add frames, text or objects. Output genuinely transparent background.

## World glyph generation prompt

Original hand-painted medieval dark fantasy game sprites, bold clean silhouettes readable at32px, weathered iron/antique gold and ivory/teal magic. Row2 left: three tiny ivory souls swirling around a small antique gold core, a collectible Soul Harvest relic. Row2 right: a small teal/ivory ice crystal within two broken antique gold prongs, a collectible Stasis relic. Every object entirely within its cell with at least10% empty margin. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background, no diffuse haze, no grey, no gradient, no cloud, no cast shadow, no framed icons, no medallions, no border, no letters/text/numbers. Each object fully detached and isolated. Four distinct sprites, not a mockup.
